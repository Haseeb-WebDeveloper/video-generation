# Video Ideas — Top X Ranking Series (V2)

## Why V1 died

The previous list (largest empires, deepest holes, mass extinctions, lethal substances) was *educational*. Educational topics get views when wrapped in documentary production — narration, custom 3D, archive footage, narrative arcs. A 4-minute Remotion ranking video with auto-fetched Wikipedia photos cannot win on "interesting" alone. We were competing in the wrong lane.

The lane that *does* work for a template-driven ranking format: **topics where the thumbnail face + title + number creates an immediate "what?!" reaction.** Money won, money lost, scandals, falls from grace, true crime, pop-culture spending. Same templates. Completely different topics.

We're not making a teacher's video. We're making a "you won't believe who's #1" video.

---

## The three filters every new topic must pass

A topic is only allowed on this list if it passes **all three**:

1. **Thumbnail recognition test** — every entry has a face, mugshot, poster, logo, or product the viewer already knows. (Wikipedia/Commons has these for almost any famous person, brand, film, scandal.)
2. **You-can't-predict-#1 test** — "Top 20 richest people" fails (everyone knows Musk/Bezos). "Top 20 actors paid the most for one role" passes (almost nobody guesses Keanu for the Matrix sequels).
3. **Narrative tension** — money lost, scandal, secret, betrayal, comeback, heist. The number alone isn't enough; there has to be a *story* attached to each entry.

If a topic fails any one of these → not on the list, no matter how cool the data is.

---

## Format constraints (unchanged)

We have **2 templates**: `flow` (continuous side-scrolling row of covers over a still photo backdrop, best for face/poster/product visuals) and `bar` (3D pillars sized by value, best for $ / count comparisons where the number is the star).

Most V2 ideas are tagged `bar` because the universal lane is "X dollars" — but for entries where the *face/poster* sells the click (actors, divorces, mugshots), `flow` wins.

---

## Anti-skip principles applied to every script

1. **Tease #1 in the first 5 seconds** — blurred image + value, then "we'll get there."
2. **Reveal #1 last.** Never count up *and* down.
3. **Drop the gap.** "And now the gap explodes" — works extra hard for money topics where the curve is exponential.
4. **One surprise per 5 ranks.** Plant unexpected entries so viewers stay to see who else they missed.
5. **First-comment pin a "did we miss anyone?" question** — engagement signal.

## LANE 1 — Money Lost / Fall from Grace

The single most viral framing on YouTube: "He had $X, now he has nothing." Mugshot/portrait thumbnails crush CTR. Bar template fits naturally — peak fortune vs. final net worth, or amount lost.

### L1.1. Top 20 Athletes Who Lost Their Entire Fortune
- **Template:** `flow` (faces) or `bar` ($ lost) — recommend `flow`.
- **Hook:** "Mike Tyson made $400M boxing. He filed for bankruptcy owing $23M. He's not even #1."
- **Why it pops:** Recognizable faces (Tyson, Iverson, Antoine Walker, Vince Young, Lawrence Taylor, Evander Holyfield, Scottie Pippen, Allen Iverson, Curt Schilling, Mark Brunell, Latrell Sprewell). Drama-stacked entries. Universal "how is this even possible" reaction.
- **Source:** SI/ESPN bankruptcy filings, court records, Wikipedia athlete biographies.
- **Surprise at #1:** Mike Tyson ($400M earned → bankrupt) or Floyd Mayweather frame-flip if including IRS debt despite high earnings.

### L1.2. Top 20 Lottery Winners Who Went Broke
- **Template:** `bar` — peak winnings vs final net worth.
- **Hook:** "Jack Whittaker won $314M in 2002. Within 5 years he'd buried his granddaughter and lost everything."
- **Why it pops:** Schadenfreude + cautionary tale. Every entry is a documentary-tier story. Genuinely shocking that this happens 70% of the time.
- **Source:** Lottery commission records, news archives, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Jack Whittaker ($314M, lost it all + family deaths) or Evelyn Adams (won lottery twice, died broke in a trailer).

### L1.3. Top 20 Most Expensive Celebrity Divorces in History
- **Template:** `flow` (couple portraits) or `bar` ($ settlement).
- **Hook:** "Jeff Bezos's divorce cost him $38 billion. One divorce cost more."
- **Why it pops:** Tabloid + business audience overlap. Bezos/MacKenzie, Bill/Melinda Gates, Murdoch, Spielberg/Irving, Tiger Woods, Michael Jordan, Mel Gibson — all instantly recognizable. Numbers go to the billions.
- **Source:** Court filings, Forbes coverage, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Bezos/MacKenzie ($38B) or Alec Wildenstein (1999, $2.5B in 90s money = ~$5B today, the "richest divorce in modern history" headline).

### L1.4. Top 20 Fortunes Wiped Out Overnight
- **Template:** `bar` — $ lost in a single week.
- **Hook:** "Sam Bankman-Fried went from $26B to $0 in a single week. He's not the biggest fall."
- **Why it pops:** Recent + recognizable. SBF, Adam Neumann (WeWork, $13B), Elizabeth Holmes (Theranos, $4.5B), Do Kwon (Terra/Luna, $60B vaporized), Changpeng Zhao, Sergey Mavrodi. Plus historical: Bernie Madoff, Jordan Belfort, Bernard Ebbers.
- **Source:** Forbes "real-time billionaires" historical snapshots, SEC filings, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Do Kwon / Terra Luna ($60B market cap to $0 in 72 hours) or SBF if framed as personal net worth.

### L1.5. Top 20 Musicians Who Died Broke Despite Selling Millions
- **Template:** `flow` (faces).
- **Hook:** "MC Hammer earned $33M from one album. He filed for bankruptcy 5 years later."
- **Why it pops:** Combines music nostalgia + tragedy. Hammer, TLC (bankrupt at peak of sales), Toni Braxton (twice), Meatloaf, Marvin Gaye, Billie Holiday, Jerry Lee Lewis, Tupac (debts to Death Row), Lauryn Hill, Dee Dee Ramone.
- **Source:** Court bankruptcy filings, music industry post-mortems, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** MC Hammer or TLC (sold 23M albums, declared bankruptcy at the peak).

### L1.6. Top 20 Child Stars Who Lost Everything
- **Template:** `flow` (then-and-now faces).
- **Hook:** "Macaulay Culkin made $17M by age 14. By 25 he was a recluse. He's not even the saddest story."
- **Why it pops:** Universal recognition, before/after thumbnails crush CTR. Culkin, Gary Coleman, Corey Haim, Lindsay Lohan, Amanda Bynes, Aaron Carter, Edward Furlong, Jake Lloyd (Anakin), Mara Wilson, Danielle Fishel.
- **Source:** E! True Hollywood Story archives, news, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Gary Coleman (earned $18M by age 12, died with $50k) or Corey Haim.
- **Note:** Frame carefully — focus on financial/career arc, not personal tragedy, to keep monetization clean.

## LANE 2 — Insane Wealth / Things Billionaires Bought

"He spent $X on Y" is the second most clickable framing in this format. `flow` template — products, yachts, mansions, art are visual artifacts.

### L2.1. Top 20 Most Expensive Things Ever Sold at Auction
- **Template:** `flow` (the object itself).
- **Hook:** "The Salvator Mundi sold for $450M to a Saudi prince. Three things sold for more."
- **Why it pops:** Visual spectacle — paintings, watches, cars, sculptures, baseball cards, dinosaur skeletons. Mix of recognizable (Da Vinci, Picasso) with WTF (a T. rex skeleton for $32M).
- **Source:** Christie's, Sotheby's, RM Sotheby's, Heritage Auctions records.
- **Surprise at #1:** Salvator Mundi ($450M, 2017) or the Leonardo Codex Hammer ($30M to Bill Gates in 1994, ~$70M adj.) — depends on inclusion rules.

### L2.2. Top 20 Most Expensive Yachts Ever Built
- **Template:** `flow` (aerial yacht shots) or `bar` ($ cost).
- **Hook:** "Roman Abramovich's Eclipse cost $1.5 billion. There's a yacht that cost more."
- **Why it pops:** Wealth porn at peak. Each yacht has an owner viewers want to gawk at (Abramovich, Bezos's Koru with the masts that nearly broke Rotterdam, MBS's Serene, Allen's Octopus). Drone shots are spectacular thumbnails.
- **Source:** SuperYacht Times, BOAT International, Wikipedia per-vessel articles.
- **Surprise at #1:** History Supreme (claimed $4.8B, mostly bunk) or — playing it honest — Eclipse / Azzam (~$600M) / MBS's Serene (~$500M).

### L2.3. Top 20 Weirdest Things Billionaires Have Bought
- **Template:** `flow` (the object).
- **Hook:** "Jeff Bezos spent $42M building a 10,000-year clock inside a mountain. It's not the weirdest thing on this list."
- **Why it pops:** Pure WTF curation. Musk buying Twitter for $44B, Ellison buying 98% of Lanai (an entire Hawaiian island), Branson's Necker, Bezos's clock, Russian oligarchs' gold-plated guns, Trump's gold toilet, Mukesh Ambani's $400M wedding.
- **Source:** Forbes / Bloomberg billionaire profiles, news archives.
- **Surprise at #1:** Larry Ellison buying 98% of the Hawaiian island of Lanai ($300M, 2012) or Musk's $44B Twitter buy.

### L2.4. Top 20 Most Expensive Sneakers Ever Sold
- **Template:** `flow` (shoe photos).
- **Hook:** "A pair of Kanye's Nike Air Yeezy 1 prototypes sold for $1.8M. There's a pair worth more."
- **Why it pops:** Sneaker culture is massive (Gen Z + Millennial), every entry has a celebrity story (MJ's game-worn Jordans, Kanye's Yeezys, Kobe's Hall of Fame shoes). Visual product = perfect flow template.
- **Source:** Sotheby's sneaker auctions, StockX, Goldin records.
- **Surprise at #1:** Michael Jordan's "The Last Dance" Game 2 worn-Jordans ($8.03M, 2023) or Kanye's grammy-worn Yeezys ($1.8M).

### L2.5. Top 20 Most Expensive Trading Cards Ever Sold (Pokémon + Sports)
- **Template:** `flow` (the card art).
- **Hook:** "Logan Paul wore a $5.275M Pokémon card around his neck at the Mayweather fight."
- **Why it pops:** Pokémon TCG resurgence + sports card boom. Mickey Mantle '52 ($12.6M, 2022), Honus Wagner T206 ($7.25M), Pikachu Illustrator ($5.275M to Logan Paul), Magic the Gathering Black Lotus.

### L2.6. Top 20 Most Expensive Homes in the World

## LANE 3 — Pop Culture Money

Movies, music, gaming, TV. Universal recognition + counterintuitive #1s. `flow` template is the natural fit (posters, faces, album covers).

### L3.1. Top 20 Actors Paid the Most for a Single Role
- **Template:** `flow` (actor headshots).
- **Hook:** "Robert Downey Jr. made $75M for Avengers Endgame. Three actors made more for one movie."
- **Why it pops:** Every entry is a face viewers know. Counterintuitive: Keanu reportedly $130M+ for Matrix sequels (backend), Sandra Bullock $70M for Gravity (backend), Cruise $100M+ for Top Gun Maverick, Bruce Willis $100M for Sixth Sense (gross participation), Will Smith $35M Bad Boys 3.
- **Source:** Variety, Hollywood Reporter, Forbes.
- **Surprise at #1:** Keanu Reeves on Matrix Reloaded + Revolutions (backend = ~$250M total across both) or Sandra Bullock on Gravity (~$70M backend on a single film).

### L3.2. Top 20 Highest-Grossing Concert Tours of All Time
- **Template:** `bar` — gross in $ millions (inflation-adjusted).
- **Hook:** "Taylor Swift's Eras Tour grossed $2 billion. Three tours grossed more (after inflation)."
- **Why it pops:** Music nostalgia + recency. Eras Tour, Beyoncé Renaissance, U2 360, Ed Sheeran ÷, Rolling Stones A Bigger Bang, Pink Floyd Division Bell, Elton John Farewell.
- **Source:** Billboard Boxscore, Pollstar.
- **Surprise at #1:** Taylor Swift Eras Tour ($2.07B nominal) — broke every previous record. If inflation-adjusted, U2 360 ($736M nominal, ~$1.05B adj) competes.

### L3.3. Top 20 Biggest Box Office Bombs in History
- **Template:** `flow` (movie posters) or `bar` ($ lost).
- **Hook:** "Disney lost $200M on John Carter. Three movies lost more."
- **Why it pops:** Schadenfreude + recognizable IP. John Carter, The Lone Ranger, Mars Needs Moms, 47 Ronin, The Marvels, Lightyear, Cats, Cleopatra (1963 — still on top inflation-adjusted), Heaven's Gate, Pluto Nash.
- **Source:** Variety, Deadline post-mortems, The Numbers.
- **Surprise at #1:** Cleopatra (1963, $44M budget = $400M+ today, nearly bankrupted Fox) or The Marvels (2023, ~$237M loss).

### L3.4. Top 20 Most Expensive Video Games Ever Made
- **Template:** `bar` — development cost.
- **Hook:** "Star Citizen has raised $700 million and is still not finished after 12 years."
- **Why it pops:** Gaming audience is huge and engaged. Star Citizen, GTA VI (rumored $1B+), Cyberpunk 2077 ($316M), Destiny ($500M), Red Dead Redemption 2 ($540M), Spider-Man 2 PS5 ($300M).
- **Source:** Bloomberg games reporting, leaked court filings (Insomniac hack, Take-Two filings).
- **Surprise at #1:** Star Citizen ($700M+ and counting) or GTA VI if rumored $1B+ holds.

### L3.5. Top 20 Most-Watched TV Moments in History (Live Audience)
- **Template:** `bar` — live viewers in millions.
- **Hook:** "The M*A*S*H finale had 105 million Americans watching in 1983. One TV moment had 6× more."
- **Why it pops:** Nostalgia + WTF #1. Apollo 11 moon landing (650M global), Princess Diana funeral (2.5B), 9/11 coverage (~2B), 2008 Olympics opening (~1B). M*A*S*H, Cheers, Friends, Roots, Super Bowl finales.
- **Source:** Nielsen archives, BBC, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Princess Diana's funeral (2.5B viewers, 1997) or Apollo 11 (650M live).

### L3.6. Top 20 Highest-Paid YouTubers of All Time
- **Template:** `flow` (creator faces).
- **Hook:** "Ryan Kaji made $30 million reviewing toys at age 8. There's a YouTuber who made 3× more in a single year."
- **Why it pops:** Gen Z immediate-recognition. MrBeast ($82M est 2024), Ryan's World, Jake Paul, Logan Paul, Markiplier, PewDiePie, KSI, Dude Perfect, Like Nastya, Vlad and Niki.
- **Source:** Forbes Highest-Paid YouTubers annual list.
- **Surprise at #1:** MrBeast ($82M, 2023) or — historical peak — Ryan's World ($29M at age 8).

## LANE 4 — True Crime / Heists / Scams

Netflix-doc adjacency. Mugshots and "missing" posters crush CTR. `bar` template = $ stolen or scammed.

### L4.1. Top 20 Biggest Heists in History (by $ Stolen)
- **Template:** `bar` — $ stolen, inflation-adjusted.
- **Hook:** "Thieves tunneled into a Brazilian bank and stole $69M cash in 2005. Three heists stole more."
- **Why it pops:** Every entry is a movie-tier story. Banco Central Brazil ($69M tunnel job), Antwerp Diamond Heist ($100M), Brink's-Mat (£26M, 1983, "Crime of the Century"), Gardner Museum art theft ($500M, still missing), Dunbar Armored ($18.9M), Saddam's $1B Central Bank withdrawal (the largest single bank robbery — by Saddam himself, pre-invasion).
- **Source:** FBI archives, Interpol, Wikipedia heist articles.
- **Surprise at #1:** Saddam Hussein's $1B Iraqi Central Bank withdrawal (March 18, 2003) — the largest bank robbery ever, by the country's own dictator.

### L4.2. Top 20 Largest Ponzi Schemes in History
- **Template:** `bar` — $ scammed.
- **Hook:** "Bernie Madoff ran a $65 billion scam for 30 years undetected. There's been a bigger one."
- **Why it pops:** Recognizable names, recent (SBF/FTX 2022). Madoff ($65B), Allen Stanford ($7B), Tom Petters ($3.65B), Scott Rothstein ($1.2B), Charles Ponzi himself (the original). FTX if you count it as Ponzi-adjacent ($8B+).
- **Source:** SEC complaints, court filings, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Bernie Madoff ($65B fictitious value) — still the largest.

### L4.3. Top 20 Biggest Crypto Collapses Ever
- **Template:** `bar` — market cap evaporated.
- **Hook:** "Terra Luna lost $60 billion in 72 hours in May 2022. One collapse was bigger."
- **Why it pops:** Recent + technical drama. Terra/Luna ($60B), FTX ($32B + $8B customer funds), Mt. Gox (850k BTC, 2014), Celsius, Three Arrows Capital, BitConnect, OneCoin ($4B fake coin), QuadrigaCX (founder died with the keys).
- **Source:** Chainalysis reports, court filings, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Terra/Luna ($60B evaporated in May 2022) or FTX if you bundle exchange + token + Alameda.

### L4.4. Top 20 Biggest Art Heists Still Unsolved
- **Template:** `flow` (the missing paintings themselves).
- **Hook:** "13 paintings were stolen from the Gardner Museum in 1990, worth $500 million. They've never been found."
- **Why it pops:** Mystery + masterpiece visuals. Vermeer, Rembrandt, Caravaggio thefts still open. Gardner Museum (1990), Caravaggio's Nativity (Palermo 1969), van Gogh "View of the Sea at Scheveningen", Munch's "The Scream" (recovered).
- **Source:** FBI Art Crime Team, Interpol Works of Art database.
- **Surprise at #1:** Gardner Museum 1990 — Vermeer's "The Concert" alone is worth $250M.

### L4.5. Top 20 Most Successful Con Artists in History
- **Template:** `flow` (mugshot portraits) or `bar` ($ scammed).
- **Hook:** "Anna Delvey conned New York's elite out of $275,000. She's not even the smallest fish on this list."
- **Why it pops:** Netflix doc audience — Inventing Anna, The Tinder Swindler, Bad Vegan, The Dropout. Recognizable: Anna Delvey, Frank Abagnale (Catch Me If You Can), Charles Ponzi, Bernie Madoff, Elizabeth Holmes, Billy McFarland (Fyre Festival), Simon Leviev.
- **Source:** Court records, documentaries, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Bernie Madoff ($65B) or — if filtering for "true con artist" (not fund manager) — Victor Lustig (the man who sold the Eiffel Tower, twice).

### L4.6. Top 20 Largest Data Breaches in History
- **Template:** `bar` — accounts compromised.
- **Hook:** "Yahoo lost 3 billion user accounts in one breach. One breach was bigger."
- **Why it pops:** Tech-savvy audience, recent recency (Optus, Equifax, MoveIt, Marriott). Tech-CPM, family-safe.
- **Source:** Have I Been Pwned, IBM data breach reports, Wikipedia.
- **Surprise at #1:** Yahoo 2013 (3B accounts) or Aadhaar leak (1.1B Indian citizens — but lower-bound).
